Features

Kept simple for the
busiest of organizers.

Pager is deliberately narrow in scope. We aim to provide a one-way broadcast, done exceptionally well.

Multi-channel broadcasting
Separate channels for separate audiences. Attendees subscribe only to what matters to them — cutting through notification fatigue.
Lock-screen push notifications
Messages land on attendees' lock screens the moment you send them. No need to have the app open — it works just like any other notification.
No app to download
Pager installs as a Progressive Web App — a one-time prompt, no app store. Attendees are subscribed before the queue even moves.
QR code onboarding
One QR code handles the whole subscriber flow. Print it anywhere — signage, lanyards, your event website. Scanning is subscribing.
